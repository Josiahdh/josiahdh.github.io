### Featured at GDC 2026, Masterworks of Horror is a real-time PvP card-battler created in USC’s Advanced Games Project program with a team of over 55 students and volunteers.

{% include embed-video.html id="LtyPOjkhrtY" %}

As lead producer, I managed a team of 55+ students and volunteers, tracking tasks and ensuring features developed from conception to implementation. However, beyond management, I had the privilege of supporting an incredible team of friends. This involved leading trailer editing, assisting in creating slide decks, and providing creative direction.
